Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

CloudDrive ("the App") is an open-source desktop application that helps you manage multiple Google Drive accounts from a single interface. This Privacy Policy describes how the App handles your information.

2. Data We Collect

CloudDrive operates entirely on your local machine. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. Specifically:

  • Google Account Data: OAuth tokens and account metadata are stored locally on your machine using your operating system's secure keyring (e.g., GNOME Keyring, KWallet) and never transmitted to any third party.
  • File Data: Your Google Drive files are accessed directly via the Google Drive API. File contents are cached locally on your machine in ~/.cache/clouddrive/ for performance and are never sent to any server other than Google's.
  • Configuration: App settings are stored locally in ~/.config/clouddrive/config.toml.
  • Metadata Cache: File metadata is cached locally in an SQLite database at ~/.local/share/clouddrive/.

3. Google API Usage

CloudDrive uses the Google Drive API and Google OAuth 2.0 for authentication. Your usage of these services is governed by Google's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. The App requests the following OAuth scopes:

  • drive — Full access to your Google Drive files
  • userinfo.email — Access to your email address for account identification

4. Data Storage & Security

  • OAuth refresh tokens are stored in your system's secure keyring with restricted permissions.
  • Sensitive fields in logs are automatically redacted (tokens, secrets, API keys).
  • All communication with Google's APIs uses HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting is collected or sent anywhere.

5. Third-Party Services

The App communicates only with Google's API servers (googleapis.com) and your local filesystem. No other third-party services are contacted.

6. Data Deletion

You can remove all locally stored data at any time by:

  • Removing accounts through the Settings page (which clears tokens from the keyring)
  • Deleting the configuration directory: ~/.config/clouddrive/
  • Deleting the data directory: ~/.local/share/clouddrive/
  • Deleting the cache directory: ~/.cache/clouddrive/

7. Children's Privacy

CloudDrive is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above and distributed with updated versions of the application.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
